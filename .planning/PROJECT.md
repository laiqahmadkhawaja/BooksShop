# Kitab Ghar & Stationery - Bookshop POS & ERP System

## Project Overview
Kitab Ghar is a specialized Bookshop Point of Sale (POS) and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) web system developed in procedural PHP with Bootstrap 5, MySQL (PDO), and vanilla JavaScript.

### Core Modules
- **Point of Sale (POS)**: High-speed barcode scanning, cart calculation, discounts, split/credit payment, thermal invoice generation.
- **Product Catalog**: Multi-category support (Books, Stationery, Toys, etc.), ISBN/Barcode management, dynamic pricing tiers (Retail/Wholesale).
- **Suppliers & Purchases**: Inward goods receiving, supplier billing & credit ledgers.
- **Customers & Khata**: Registered accounts, credit purchase limits, ledger statements, receivables tracking.
- **Accounting & Financial Accounts**: Cash drawers, bank accounts, digital wallets, cash journal, profit & loss, trial balance.
- **School Syllabus Packages**: Class-wise book packs and bundles.
- **Warehouse & Inventory**: Multi-warehouse stock tracking, stock valuation, inter-branch/godown transfers, low-stock threshold monitoring.
